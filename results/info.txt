Interpreting results- 
2S = exclusively 2-state STICA; >2S = more than 2 state STICA.

Results refer to the computed runtime of trials. 
Trials are split into two categories- STICA Only trials and Genetic Algorithm trials.
Runtimes are recorded in seconds.

In STICA Only trials, results are indicated by the variables *data* (2S) or *dataincomp* (>2S). 
Each result is recorded as a tuple ((grid size, iterations), runtime).
In >2S results, the tuple is ((grid size, iterations, states), runtime).

In Genetic Algorithm trials (generate_data.ipynb), results are indicated by the variable *timelogs*.
The results are recorded as an array of runtimes, with the first element corresponding to the runtime of the first iteration.
Following a comment indicating "GPU Naive-Comparable Trial Timelogs", either on STICA2S or NSTICA, the corresponding results are actually the last 320 values of the array printed below the comment.
